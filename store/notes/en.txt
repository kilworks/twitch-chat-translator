New option, off by default: translate a channel only after you switch it on. Turn on "Translate a channel only after I switch it on in the bar" in the Display tab. Every channel then stays untranslated until you press play on the bar, or Resume in the popup, and the choice lasts for that tab only.

Chinese and Japanese chat reads better. Short lines written only with Han characters are no longer hidden or mistaken for the other language, Taiwanese and mainland Chinese are told apart far more often, and a mostly Cantonese chat is recognised, so its lines are translated instead of skipped.

Cloud first is now the default engine mode, because blind judges scored the on-device engine worse than Google in every language it served. If you were on On-device first, you are moved once to cloud first, so your messages go to an online translation service first; you can switch back in the options and the choice then holds.

Safer on Chrome: your settings and your DeepL key are closed to the scripts that run inside Twitch pages, and importing a settings file can no longer change your usage statistics choice, your MyMemory email or your Lingva address.

Failed lines say "Translation failed" in your language instead of a code, and a translation service that is cooling down is no longer asked again on every batch.

The About tab now explains the research behind the translator and links the public repositories.
