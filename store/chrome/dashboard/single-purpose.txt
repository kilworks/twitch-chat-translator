Twitch Chat Translator does one thing: it translates chat on twitch.tv. Incoming
messages get their translation rendered under, inside or in place of the
original, and the message the user is typing gets a preview in the language the
channel broadcasts in. It works on the chat of www.twitch.tv, m.twitch.tv and
the stream manager on dashboard.twitch.tv. Every permission below exists to
serve that, and the extension is inert on every other site.
